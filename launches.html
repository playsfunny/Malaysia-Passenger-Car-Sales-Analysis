Proton eMas 7 PHEV 上市：大马最便宜插混车，早鸟价RM105,800起

Proton eMas 7 PHEV launches as Malaysia's cheapest plug-in hybrid, from RM105,800

2026/02/04CBU（後转CKD） RM105,800 – RM125,800

这是Proton在14周内的第四款新车（继10月eMas 5、11月Saga MC3与1月X70 MC3之後），也是eMas家族首款配备引擎与排气管的车型——eMas 7 PHEV正式在大马上市。三种车型定价为：Prime RM109,800、Premium RM123,800、Premium Plus RM129,800（均为未含保险上路价），首5,000名顾客可享RM4,000早鸟回扣，实际到手价降至RM105,800、RM119,800与RM125,800，成功从RM130,000的Chery Tiggo 7 PHEV手中夺下"大马最便宜插混车"称号。此外还附赠5年每月2GB免费流量（价值RM1,000）、免费7kW家用充电桩（价值RM1,500）及eMas Care保险赠品，全部加起来价值达RM7,500。

动力系统上，Prime与Premium搭载1.5升自然进气BHE15-DFN四缸引擎（99PS/125Nm）配合218PS/262Nm前置电机，综合输出262PS/262Nm，前轮驱动；两者共用18.4kWh CATL磷酸铁锂电池，提供83公里WLTP纯电续航，943公里综合续航，直流快充最高30kW（30–80%需时20分钟内），0–100km/h为8秒。顶配Premium Plus换上29.8kWh Geely Aegis短刀片磷酸铁锂电池，纯电续航提升至146公里，综合续航996公里，快充提升至60kW（30–80%仅需16分钟），加速稍快为8.2秒——值得一提，大马是全球市场中唯一取得2026年式Galaxy Starship 7大电池版本的地区。车身尺寸4,740×1,940×1,685毫米，轴距2,755毫米，较eMas 7 EV车型分别加长125毫米、加宽39毫米、增高15毫米。座舱配备Flyme OS车机系统（支援马来文）、10.2英寸仪表、2.5K 15.4英寸中控屏幕，Premium以上车型另有13.8英寸抬头显示、16喇叭Flyme Sound音响（含头枕喇叭）与七气囊（含中央气囊）；驾驶模式提供纯电、混动与动力三种。质保为6年不限里程整车保固，另享8年/16万公里高压电池与相关组件保固。

This is Proton's fourth launch in just 14 weeks — following October's eMas 5, November's Saga MC3 and January's X70 MC3 — and the first eMas model with an engine and an exhaust pipe: the eMas 7 PHEV has officially launched in Malaysia. The three variants are priced at RM109,800 for the Prime, RM123,800 for the Premium and RM129,800 for the Premium Plus (on-the-road without insurance), with the first 5,000 customers getting a RM4,000 early-bird rebate that brings the effective prices down to RM105,800, RM119,800 and RM125,800 — officially snatching the title of Malaysia's cheapest plug-in hybrid from the RM130,000 Chery Tiggo 7 PHEV. Buyers also get five years of free 2GB monthly data (worth RM1,000), a free 7kW home charger (worth RM1,500) and eMas Care insurance perks, together worth up to RM7,500.

The Prime and Premium use a 1.5-litre naturally aspirated BHE15-DFN four-cylinder engine (99 PS/125 Nm) paired with a 218 PS/262 Nm front motor for a combined 262 PS and 262 Nm, driving the front wheels; both share an 18.4 kWh CATL LFP battery giving 83 km of WLTP EV range and 943 km combined range, with DC fast charging up to 30 kW (30-80% in under 20 minutes) and a 0-100 km/h time of 8 seconds. The range-topping Premium Plus swaps in a 29.8 kWh Geely Aegis short-blade LFP battery, lifting EV range to 146 km and combined range to 996 km, with faster 60 kW DC charging (30-80% in under 16 minutes) and a slightly quicker 8.2-second sprint — notably, Malaysia is the only global market to get the larger battery from the 2026-model-year Galaxy Starship 7. Dimensions are 4,740 x 1,940 x 1,685 mm on a 2,755 mm wheelbase, respectively 125 mm longer, 39 mm wider and 15 mm taller than the eMas 7 EV. The cabin runs Flyme OS (with Bahasa Melayu support), a 10.2-inch instrument cluster and a 2.5K 15.4-inch touchscreen, with the Premium and above adding a 13.8-inch head-up display, a 16-speaker Flyme Sound system (including headrest speakers) and seven airbags (including a centre airbag); drive modes are Pure (EV-only), Hybrid and Power. Warranty covers six years unlimited-mileage on the vehicle, plus eight years/160,000 km on the high-voltage battery and components.

Exterior updates add a Shadowline gloss-black package (grille, rear diffuser, side skirts, mirrors, roof rails and M Lights Shadowline headlamps), with the 19-inch M twin-spoke 871M alloys carried over. Inside, the seats get softer foam and a 15 mm longer cushion base, paired with Perforated Veganza faux leather in Castanea red, with Driving Assistant Plus (adaptive cruise with stop-and-go, steering and lane control assist, and speed limit recognition) standard here versus an optional ConnectedDrive-store item on the standard-wheelbase car. A panoramic glass roof is new — even the discontinued xDrive30 didn't get one — while other kit, including the Curved Display combo (10.25-inch instrument panel plus 10.7-inch touchscreen), 12-speaker 205-watt Harman Kardon sound system, powered tailgate and Parking Assistant Plus camera system, carries over unchanged. Colours are Mineral White, Brooklyn Grey, Skyscraper Grey and Carbon Black.
