About Malaysia's Car Market

The Malaysian passenger car market is one of Southeast Asia's most dynamic, with ~870k registrations in 2025 and steady growth. Local brands Perodua and Proton lead, while Japanese brands (Toyota, Honda, Mazda) face rising competition from Chinese brands (Chery, BYD, GWM, Neta, Xpeng, Leapmotor), and EV (BEV) / hybrid (HEV/PHEV) penetration is rising fast.

This tool cross-analyzes Malaysia car sales from JPJ official registration data so you can quickly see which brands sell best, which states drive volume, how the fuel mix is evolving, and how model prices are distributed — no technical background needed.

  • Rankings, YoY growth, share and heatmaps
  • TOP35 brands after-sales policies (warranty/service cost) reference
  • Nationwide dealer map + isochrones + business-district clusters

FAQ

About data sources, coverage and usage

What is the source of this Malaysia car sales data?

Monthly registration data from the Malaysian Road Transport Department (JPJ), enriched with MAA and paultan.org model specs and prices. Brand, model, state and fuel dimensions come from official records.

Which years of car sales data does the tool cover?

Monthly data from January 2021 to July 2026 (67 months), continuously updated with each JPJ monthly release.

Which car brands are included?

All mainstream passenger-car brands (TOP50): Perodua, Proton, Toyota, Honda, Mazda, Chery, BYD, GWM, Neta, Xpeng, Leapmotor and more.

Can I view sales distribution by state?

Yes. The tool shows sales across Malaysia's 14 states and federal territories, cross-filterable by brand, model and fuel type.

Does the tool support English?

Yes. Built-in Chinese/English switching covers all analysis dimensions and charts.

How do I use this tool?

No registration or login needed — click the "Open Analysis Tool" button. Single-file app with embedded data, no backend required.

How big is Malaysia's car market overall?

Total registrations exceeded 4.32 million in 2021-2026; annual sales grew from ~530k (2021) to ~870k (2025), with ~489k in Jan-Jul 2026. Perodua and Proton have long held the top two spots.
